Privacy Policy for Bill Tracker

Effective date: April 2, 2026

Bill Tracker is provided by Signal & Shield Solutions.

Bill Tracker is designed to help users track bills, due dates, pay intervals, and related budgeting information.

Information We Collect

Bill Tracker does not require account creation or sign-in.

Bill Tracker does not transmit your bill data to our servers.

Bill Tracker stores the information you enter directly on your device, such as:

  • bill names
  • due dates
  • payment amounts
  • paycheck planning values
  • balances and paid status
  • exported file contents you choose to create

How Your Information Is Used

The information entered into Bill Tracker is used only to provide the app\u2019s planning, tracking, and export features on your device.

Data Sharing

Signal & Shield Solutions does not sell your personal data.

Signal & Shield Solutions does not share your bill data with third parties because the app currently stores this information locally on your device only.

Exported Data

If you use the export feature, the app creates a file based on the information you entered. That exported file is stored or shared only according to the actions you choose on your device.

Advertising and Purchases

Bill Tracker does not currently show ads or offer in-app purchases.

If ads or in-app purchases are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are made available.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you edit, delete, clear, or uninstall the app.

If you uninstall the app, locally stored app data may be removed by your device or operating system.

If you use any in-app reset or clear-data feature, the affected stored data will be overwritten or deleted from the app\u2019s local storage.

Security

Because Bill Tracker currently stores data locally on your device, protecting access to your device helps protect your information. We recommend using your device\u2019s built-in security features such as a lock screen, PIN, password, or biometrics.

Children

Bill Tracker is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Signal & Shield Solutions may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted at the same public URL.
