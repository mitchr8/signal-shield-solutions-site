Planning coverage

How many security cameras do you actually need?

The honest answer is that it depends on the property, but there are practical starting points. The goal is full coverage of the spots that matter with clear, usable footage, not the highest camera count a salesperson can sell you.

Typical starting points

Most homes Four to eight cameras: front door, driveway, back yard, and any blind sides.
Small business Six to sixteen: entrances, registers, storage, back doors, and parking.
Larger sites Quoted by scope, with recording retention planned to match the coverage.
Cover the entries first

Doors, gates, and driveways.

  • Every way in and out should have a clear view of faces and plates
  • Entry cameras earn their spot before decorative coverage
  • Lighting and angle matter as much as the camera itself
Kill the blind spots

Side yards, alleys, and back doors.

  • The quiet, unlit sides are where problems usually start
  • Overlap coverage so no single camera is a weak point
  • Outdoor and PoE cameras chosen for High Desert sun, wind, and heat

FAQ

Common questions before a camera install.

How many security cameras do I need?

Most homes are well covered with four to eight cameras: front door, driveway, back yard, and any blind sides. Small businesses usually start at six to sixteen depending on entrances, registers, storage, and parking. We plan coverage around your actual property so you are not paying for cameras that watch the same wall.

Do you install for both homes and businesses?

Yes. We handle residential systems, storefronts, offices, shops, and lots across the High Desert, and size the recording and retention to match.

Can you set up remote viewing on my phone?

Yes. Live view and motion alerts on your phone are part of every install, with user access set up for the right people.

Can you add to or fix an existing system?

Often, yes. We repair offline cameras, storage and recording problems, and poor placement, and we can add cameras or upgrade older gear over time.

What areas do you cover?

On-site installs are centered on Apple Valley, Victorville, Hesperia, and the wider High Desert, with larger jobs farther out quoted on request. Remote support and monitoring for existing camera systems is available for clients anywhere.
