FAQ

Questions about remote IT support.

What can you fix remotely?

Most day-to-day IT issues: slow or misbehaving computers, software and email problems, updates and security, user accounts, printers, and network settings. We connect over secure remote tools, so most fixes happen without anyone coming out.

Do you support businesses outside the High Desert?

Yes. Remote IT support and managed IT are available for small businesses anywhere in the United States. We are based in Apple Valley, California, and offer on-site visits across the High Desert as well.

Is remote support secure?

Yes. We use secure, permission-based remote tools, so you see and approve the connection, and access ends when the work is done. Ongoing plans use monitored, encrypted access.

How does pricing work?

One-off remote help can be handled per issue, and ongoing support is a flat monthly plan scaled to your number of devices and users. Plans are quoted to your setup before any work begins.

What if a problem cannot be fixed remotely?

Some hardware issues need hands on the machine. For High Desert clients we come on-site, and for clients farther out we walk you or your on-site person through it, or coordinate a local hands-on option.
