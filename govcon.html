FAQ

Do you subcontract / team with primes?

Yes. We support primes with quoting inputs, execution planning, and reliable documented delivery.

How do you ensure compliance?

We build the plan around the solicitation/contract: PWS/SOW, schedule, WD (if applicable), performance standards, and required reporting.

What helps you quote fastest?

Scope/PWS, place of performance, schedule/hours, deliverables/reporting, site access requirements, and any WD/labor constraints.

Can you support multiple service areas?

Yes. We keep capabilities broad and align delivery to the contract. Final scope is always confirmed in writing.

Do you provide documentation and reporting?

Yes. Logs, QC checklists, and status reporting are provided based on contract requirements—kept clean and invoice-ready.
