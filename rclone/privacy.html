MOLONLABE Rclone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy describes the personal MOLONLABE Rclone desktop integration's handling of data obtained through Google Drive permissions. It does not replace the privacy policies of Google, rclone, or any separately connected applications.

Google data accessed

When explicitly authorized by the account owner, rclone may access file names, metadata, folders and file contents from Google Drive. With the full Drive scope, it can also create, edit, copy, move and delete files. The scope is broad because the operator may need to work with existing Drive files across multiple personal projects.

How information is used

Google user data is used only for the owner's configured file-management operations, such as reading, transferring, organizing and backing up Drive files, or supplying approved inputs and outputs to an expressly initiated personal automation. Data is not used for advertising, sale of personal information, or training a general-purpose AI model through this integration.

Storage and sharing

rclone runs on the authorized personal computer. OAuth tokens and rclone configuration are stored locally there; file transfers and local logs may create additional copies in locations selected by the owner. This static website has no mechanism to receive Google Drive files, OAuth tokens or account contents. Separate external applications or destinations are used only when the owner explicitly configures such transfers; their own policies may apply.

Security and access

The account owner is responsible for protecting local credentials and restricting access to the authorized device. This integration is intended for owner-initiated use. Its associated website does not expose a public Drive API or remote file-browsing portal.

Retention and deletion

Google Drive retains source files according to the owner's account settings. The owner controls any copies and logs created by rclone on their computer or chosen storage destinations. The owner can remove local configuration and copies, and revoke the app's Google access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings.
