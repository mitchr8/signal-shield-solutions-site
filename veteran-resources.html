Benefits most people miss

Earned perks that quietly save real money.

These are the ones veterans are most surprised to learn they qualify for. Eligibility varies, so check each link.

Free for life

National Parks lifetime pass.

  • America the Beautiful Military/Veterans Pass — free lifetime access to national parks and federal recreation lands for veterans and Gold Star families.
Shop on base

Commissary, Exchange & MWR access.

  • Expanded base privileges — since 2020, all service-connected disabled veterans, Purple Heart recipients, former POWs, and caregivers can shop tax-free at commissaries and exchanges and use MWR facilities.
Fly standby

Space-A military flights.

  • Space-Available travel — 100% service-connected disabled veterans can fly standby on military aircraft, often for free.
Extra pension

Aid & Attendance.

  • Aid & Attendance and Housebound — an added monthly payment on top of VA pension if you need help with daily activities or are largely confined to home. Widely underused by older veterans.
Free filing

MilTax free tax software.

  • MilTax — free federal and state tax prep and filing built for the military community, with no income limit.
Counseling

Vet Centers.

  • Vet Centers — free, confidential readjustment and PTSD counseling, separate from VA hospitals, for combat veterans and their families. You do not need a VA disability rating.
Health care & disability

Care, ratings, and the PACT Act.

Enroll

VA health care.

  • Apply for VA health care — more veterans qualify than assume they do, especially after recent expansions.
  • VA dental (VADIP) — dental coverage options many veterans overlook.
Compensation

Disability benefits.

  • Disability compensation — file a new claim, add conditions, or request an increase.
  • PACT Act — expanded eligibility for burn pit, Agent Orange, and other toxic exposures. If you were denied before, it may be worth another look.
Family

Coverage for dependents.

  • CHAMPVA — health coverage for spouses and children of permanently and totally disabled veterans.
Education & training

School, trades, and tech, for you and your family.

GI Bill

Post-9/11 GI Bill.

  • GI Bill benefits — tuition, housing allowance, and books.
  • Yellow Ribbon Program — covers tuition beyond the GI Bill cap at many private and out-of-state schools.
Often better than GI Bill

Veteran Readiness & Employment.

  • VR&E (Chapter 31) — for service-connected veterans, often more generous than the GI Bill and it can be used for training, school, or starting a business. Underused.
  • DoD SkillBridge — civilian job training and internships during your final months of service.
California, big one

CalVet College Fee Waiver.

  • College Fee Waiver — waives system-wide tuition and fees at California public colleges (community colleges, CSU, UC) for eligible children and spouses of service-connected disabled or deceased veterans. Can be worth tens of thousands.
  • Fry Scholarship & DEA — education benefits for survivors and dependents.
Home & housing

Buying, adapting, and staying housed.

$0 down

VA & CalVet home loans.

  • VA home loan — no down payment, no private mortgage insurance, and the funding fee is waived if you are service-connected disabled.
  • CalVet Home Loans — California’s own veteran loan program, sometimes with competitive terms and protections.
Adapt your home

Disability housing grants.

  • SAH & SHA grants — help paying for home modifications tied to a service-connected disability.
  • Native American Direct Loan — for eligible Native American veterans on federal trust land.
Save on taxes / stay housed

Property tax & homeless support.

  • California Disabled Veterans’ Property Tax Exemption — a substantial property tax break for 100% disabled veterans and eligible surviving spouses.
  • HUD-VASH — housing vouchers plus case management for veterans facing homelessness.
Employment & business

Jobs, hiring preference, and veteran-owned business.

Jobs

Hiring preference & placement.

  • Feds Hire Vets — veterans’ preference and the federal hiring process explained.
  • VA careers & employment — job search, resume help, and career counseling.
Your business

Veteran-owned business.

  • SDVOSB / VOSB certification (SBA) — certify your veteran-owned business to compete for set-aside federal contracts.
  • Veterans Business Outreach Center — free business counseling and the Boots to Business program.
California statewide

State benefits worth claiming.

California adds real benefits on top of federal ones. CalVet is the hub for all of them.

Start here

CalVet.

  • CalVet — California Department of Veterans Affairs, the front door to every state benefit below.
  • CalVet Veterans Homes — long-term and skilled nursing care for eligible veterans.
Everyday savings

DMV & state parks.

  • California DMV veteran services — a free “VETERAN” designation on your license or ID, disabled veteran license plates, and fee waivers.
  • California state park veteran passes — free or discounted access for eligible disabled veterans and others.
Money back

Education & property tax.

  • College Fee Waiver — California public college tuition waived for eligible dependents.
  • Disabled Veterans’ Property Tax Exemption — a significant break on your home’s property tax.
Local — High Desert & San Bernardino County

Help close to home, from people who know the area.

Start with a County Veterans Service Officer. They file VA claims for you at no cost, and you should never pay anyone to file a basic claim.

File your claim free A County Veterans Service Officer (CVSO) helps you file and appeal VA claims at no charge.
Get seen locally VA care in the High Desert runs through the Loma Linda system, with a clinic serving the Victorville area.
Find community Local American Legion and VFW posts offer events, camaraderie, and claim help.
Claims help, free

San Bernardino County Veterans Affairs.

  • Find your County Veterans Service Officer — official CalVet directory; look up San Bernardino County for the office nearest the High Desert.
VA health, local

VA care in the High Desert.

  • VA Loma Linda Healthcare System — the regional VA health system for our area.
  • VA facility locator — find the nearest clinic (including the Victorville area) or Vet Center by ZIP code.
Community

Local posts & counseling.

  • Find a Vet Center — free readjustment counseling near you.
  • American Legion & VFW — find posts in Apple Valley, Victorville, and Hesperia. Call to confirm current hours.
Survivors & families

Support for spouses, children, and caregivers.

Survivors

Compensation & pension.

  • Dependency & Indemnity Compensation (DIC) — monthly benefit for eligible surviving spouses, children, and parents.
  • Survivors Pension — for eligible low-income surviving spouses and children of wartime veterans.
Caregivers

Caregiver support.

  • Family & caregiver benefits — stipends, training, health coverage, and respite for those caring for a veteran.

Account & DD-214.
