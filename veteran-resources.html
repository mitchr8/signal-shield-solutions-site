In crisis or just need to talk? Reach the Veterans Crisis Line now.

Free, confidential support, 24/7, for veterans, service members, and their families. You do not have to be enrolled in VA care.

  • Call: dial 988, then press 1
  • Text: 838255
  • Online chat: veteranscrisisline.net

Heads up: Signal & Shield Solutions is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, CalVet, or any organization listed here. Benefit rules, discounts, and amounts change often, so always confirm details on the official links. Nothing here is legal, medical, or financial advice.

Discounts, travel & everyday savings

The perks veterans leave on the table.

Free to join, and they add up fast. Verify your status once and reuse it across dozens of brands.

Travel, big savings

Armed Forces Vacation Club (AFVC).

  • Armed Forces Vacation Club — free membership for active, retired, veteran, Guard/Reserve, and Gold Star families. Week-long resort stays from around $349, plus discounts on 600,000+ hotels, cruises, and car rentals worldwide.
Gear & eyewear

Oakley Standard Issue (SI).

  • Oakley Standard Issue — free program with direct/discount pricing on Oakley eyewear and gear for military, veterans, first responders, and government. Veterans get lifetime membership.
Verify once, save everywhere

WeSalute, GovX & ID.me.

  • WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage) — a digital military ID plus year-round discounts and travel perks.
  • GovX — free exclusive pricing on hundreds of brands and gear.
  • ID.me military verification — verify once and unlock military discounts across many major retailers.
Free for life

National Parks lifetime pass.

  • America the Beautiful Military/Veterans Pass — free lifetime access to national parks and federal recreation lands for veterans and Gold Star families.
  • Tip: many retailers (home improvement, cellphone, insurance, hotels) give 10–25% off with a verified military ID. Ask everywhere.
VA benefits most people miss

Earned benefits that quietly save real money.

The ones veterans are most surprised to learn they qualify for. Eligibility varies, so check each link.

Shop on base

Commissary, Exchange & MWR.

  • Expanded base privileges — since 2020, all service-connected disabled veterans, Purple Heart recipients, former POWs, and caregivers can shop tax-free at commissaries and exchanges and use MWR facilities.
Fly standby

Space-A military flights.

  • Space-Available travel — 100% service-connected disabled veterans can fly standby on military aircraft, often for free.
Extra pension

Aid & Attendance.

  • Aid & Attendance and Housebound — an added monthly payment on top of VA pension if you need help with daily activities or are largely confined to home. Widely underused by older veterans.
Free filing

MilTax free tax software.

  • MilTax — free federal and state tax prep and filing built for the military community, with no income limit.
Health care & disability

Care, ratings, and the PACT Act.

Enroll

VA health care.

  • Apply for VA health care — more veterans qualify than assume they do, especially after recent expansions.
  • VA dental (VADIP) — dental coverage options many veterans overlook.
Compensation

Disability benefits.

  • Disability compensation — file a new claim, add conditions, or request an increase.
  • PACT Act — expanded eligibility for burn pit, Agent Orange, and other toxic exposures. If you were denied before, it may be worth another look.
Family

Coverage for dependents.

  • CHAMPVA — health coverage for spouses and children of permanently and totally disabled veterans.
  • Caregiver support — stipends, training, and health coverage for those caring for a veteran.
Mental health & peer support

Help that gets military life.

Free, confidential, and often faster than you expect. You do not need a VA rating.

Talk now

Crisis & peer lines.

  • Veterans Crisis Line — dial 988, then press 1, or text 838255. 24/7.
  • Vets4Warriors — 24/7 confidential peer support, veteran to veteran, for any problem, big or small.
Counseling

Clinics & counseling.

  • Cohen Veterans Network — low-cost, high-quality mental health clinics for post-9/11 veterans and families.
  • Give an Hour — free counseling from volunteer mental health professionals.
  • Vet Centers — free readjustment and PTSD counseling, separate from VA hospitals.
Education & scholarships

School, trades, and tech, for you and your family.

GI Bill

Post-9/11 GI Bill.

  • GI Bill benefits — tuition, housing allowance, and books.
  • Yellow Ribbon Program — covers tuition beyond the GI Bill cap at many private and out-of-state schools.
Often better than GI Bill

Training & retraining.

  • VR&E (Chapter 31) — for service-connected veterans, often more generous than the GI Bill, and it can fund training, school, or starting a business.
  • DoD SkillBridge — civilian job training and internships during your final months of service.
Free money for school

Scholarships & waivers.

  • CalVet College Fee Waiver — waives California public college tuition and fees for eligible children and spouses of service-connected disabled or deceased veterans. Can be worth tens of thousands.
  • Pat Tillman Foundation — scholarships and leadership development for veterans and military spouses.
  • Folds of Honor — scholarships for spouses and children of fallen or disabled service members.
  • Fry Scholarship & DEA — education benefits for survivors and dependents.
  • Student Veterans of America (SVA) — a national nonprofit with campus chapters for peer support and community, plus its own scholarship programs for student veterans.
Home & housing

Buying, adapting, and staying housed.

$0 down

VA & CalVet home loans.

  • VA home loan — no down payment, no PMI, and the funding fee is waived if you are service-connected disabled.
  • CalVet Home Loans — California’s own veteran loan program.
  • SAH & SHA grants — help paying for disability-related home modifications.
Free adapted homes

Homes for severely injured vets.

  • Homes For Our Troops — builds and donates specially adapted, mortgage-free homes for severely injured post-9/11 veterans.
  • Tunnel to Towers — mortgage-free and smart homes for catastrophically injured service members and Gold Star families.
  • Fisher House — free lodging for families while a veteran receives care at a VA or military hospital.
Save / stay housed

Property tax & homeless support.

  • California Disabled Veterans’ Property Tax Exemption — a substantial property tax break for 100% disabled veterans and eligible surviving spouses.
  • HUD-VASH — housing vouchers plus case management for veterans facing homelessness.
Employment & business

Careers, hiring preference, and veteran-owned business.

Free career help

Jobs & coaching.

  • Hire Heroes USA — free, personalized career coaching, resume help, and job placement.
  • Hiring Our Heroes — U.S. Chamber program connecting veterans and spouses with employers and fellowships.
  • Onward to Opportunity (IVMF) — free professional certifications and job placement.
  • Feds Hire Vets — veterans’ preference and federal hiring explained.
Your business

Start & grow a veteran business.

  • SDVOSB / VOSB certification (SBA) — certify to compete for set-aside federal contracts.
  • Veterans Business Outreach Center — free counseling and the Boots to Business program.
  • Warrior Rising — free mentoring and startup support for veteran entrepreneurs.
Veteran service organizations

Free claims help and a community that has your back.

Accredited Veterans Service Organizations file and appeal VA claims for you at no cost, and you should never pay anyone to file a basic claim. They also offer rides to appointments, events, and camaraderie.

They file for you, free DAV, VFW, American Legion, and AMVETS all have accredited service officers.
They fight for you These groups advocate for veterans’ benefits at the state and national level.
They connect you Local posts and chapters mean events, mentorship, and people who get it.
Claims & advocacy

The major VSOs.

  • Disabled American Veterans (DAV) — free claims help and free rides to VA appointments.
  • Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW) — claims help, advocacy, and emergency grants.
  • The American Legion — claims help, community, and programs nationwide.
More support

Specialized organizations.

  • AMVETS — claims help, scholarships, and career services.
  • Paralyzed Veterans of America — for veterans with spinal cord injury or disease.
  • Wounded Warrior Project — programs for post-9/11 wounded, ill, or injured veterans and families.
Emergency financial help

Short-term help when things get tight.

Grants and assistance for rent, utilities, car repairs, and other urgent needs, often turned around quickly.

Grants & aid

Emergency funds.

  • USA Cares — emergency financial assistance for veterans and military families.
  • Operation Homefront — critical financial assistance, transitional housing, and family programs.
Wounded & ill

Funds for injured vets.

  • Semper Fi & America’s Fund — immediate and lifetime financial support for combat wounded, critically ill, and injured across all branches.
  • VFW Unmet Needs — grants for basic needs during financial hardship.
Purpose, recreation & community

Stay connected and keep serving.

For a lot of veterans, the hardest part of transition is losing the mission and the team. These help you get both back.

Team & fitness

Community through activity.

  • Team RWB (Red, White & Blue) — local fitness and social events that reconnect veterans to their community.
Keep the mission

Serve again.

  • Team Rubicon — put your skills to work on disaster response and humanitarian missions.
Heal outdoors

Therapeutic recreation.

  • Project Healing Waters — fly fishing and outdoor programs for physical and emotional rehabilitation.
Free tickets

Live events, on the house.

  • Vet Tix (Veteran Tickets Foundation) — free nonprofit ticket program for concerts, sports, and other events. Register once and enter a lottery for tickets in your area; has given away more than 31 million tickets since 2008.
California statewide

State benefits worth claiming.

California adds real benefits on top of federal ones. CalVet is the hub for all of them.

Start here

CalVet.

  • CalVet — California Department of Veterans Affairs, the front door to every state benefit.
  • CalVet Veterans Homes — long-term and skilled nursing care.
Everyday savings

DMV & state parks.

  • California DMV veteran services — a free “VETERAN” designation, disabled veteran plates, and fee waivers.
  • California state park veteran passes — free or discounted access for eligible disabled veterans and others.
Money back

Education & property tax.

  • College Fee Waiver — California public college tuition waived for eligible dependents.
  • Disabled Veterans’ Property Tax Exemption — a significant break on your home’s property tax.
Local — High Desert & San Bernardino County

Help close to home, from people who know the area.

Start with a County Veterans Service Officer. They file VA claims for you at no cost, and you should never pay anyone to file a basic claim.

Get seen locally VA care in the High Desert runs through the Loma Linda system, with a clinic serving the Victorville area.
Find community Local American Legion and VFW posts offer events, camaraderie, and claim help.

Local posts & counseling.

Survivors & families

Support for spouses, children, and caregivers.

Survivors

Compensation & pension.

  • Dependency & Indemnity Compensation (DIC) — monthly benefit for eligible surviving spouses, children, and parents.
  • Survivors Pension — for eligible low-income survivors of wartime veterans.
Caregivers

Caregiver support.

  • VA family & caregiver benefits — stipends, training, health coverage, and respite.
  • Elizabeth Dole Foundation — resources and community for military and veteran caregivers.
