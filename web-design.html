FAQ

Common questions about a new website.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We handle the whole build for you: the design, the pages, the domain, and the business email. You tell us about your business and what you want customers to do, and we take it from there.

Do you only build websites for local businesses?

No. Website design is done remotely, so we build and maintain sites for clients anywhere in the country. We are based in Apple Valley and the High Desert, and local clients can also meet in person if they prefer.

Will my site work well on phones?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first, since most local customers will find you on a phone. It stays fast and easy to read on any screen.

Can you use my own domain and set up business email?

Yes. We can register or connect your domain and set up professional email at your own domain, so you look established and keep your branding consistent.

Can you update the site after it launches?

Yes. We offer ongoing updates and care so your hours, photos, services, and offers stay current without you having to touch anything.

How much does a website cost?

It is quoted by scope. A clean starter site for a local business is affordable, and larger sites with more pages or features are quoted before we begin so there are no surprises.
