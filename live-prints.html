FAQ

Quick answers about the live camera and custom prints.

Why does the camera show online when nothing is moving?

Online means the camera feed is connected. The printer may be between jobs, waiting on the next run, or not actively laying material at that exact moment.

Can I ask about a custom print?

Yes. Use the form below to send a part idea, reference image, sketch, dimensions, or quantity and we can follow up with pricing or next steps.

What helps with a quote?

The most useful details are what the part is for, approximate size, quantity, material if you know it, and when you need it.

Can I ask when the next live run will be up?

Yes. Use the contact form and say you want to know when the next print will be running live.
