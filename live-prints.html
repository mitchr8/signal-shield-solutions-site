FAQ

Quick answers about the live stream and print inquiries.

When does the stream show online?

The live page only shows online when the printer is in an active print state and the relay is publishing the camera feed.

Can I ask about the exact part on the screen?

Yes. Use the current print card or a product card in the gallery and the inquiry form will be prefilled for you.

What helps with a quote?

The most useful details are what the part is for, approximate size, quantity, material if you know it, and when you need it.

Can I request a custom version of a previous print?

Yes. The gallery is meant as a starting point. Use the part you like, then mention the size, quantity, or changes you want.

Ask about a print, quote, or custom version.

Pick the current print or a previous product card, then send the extra details that matter for pricing.

  • A file, photo, sketch, or short description of the part
  • Approximate size or quantity if you know it
  • Any deadline, finish, or material preference that matters
